# Heady's Lumen: ShaderPack_PhotonFork

The companion shader pack for **Heady's Lumen: ShaderLights** (mod version 1.5.0 or newer).

**Install:** download `Heady's Lumen - ShaderPack_PhotonFork.zip` from this folder (click the file, then the download
button) and drop it, still zipped, into `.minecraft/shaderpacks`. Pick it in Iris' shader pack menu.

What it adds to Photon:
- Held lights in your exact colour and brightness, flickering when the mod says so.
- Placed lights in your exact colour and brightness, flickering like a flame (the pack animates it, no extra cost).
- Coloured lights switched on by default.
